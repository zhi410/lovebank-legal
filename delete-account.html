刪除 LoveBank 帳號與資料

適用 App：LoveBank 情侶專屬虛擬網銀（開發者：LoveBank）

方法一：在 App 內自行刪除（立即生效）

  1. 開啟 LoveBank 並登入。
  2. 點下方分頁「我的」→「帳號管理」。
  3. 點「永久刪除帳號與所有關聯資料」，再次確認即可。

方法二：來信申請刪除（不需安裝 App）

請用你註冊 LoveBank 的 Email 寄信到 zhichun0410@gmail.com，主旨寫「LoveBank 刪除帳號申請」。我們確認是帳號本人後，會在 7 個工作天內完成刪除並回信通知。

會刪除的資料

  • 帳號資料：Email、暱稱、頭像、登入憑證
  • 你所屬共享金庫的所有內容：點數、交易明細、價目表、兌換券、紅包、時光膠囊、心情天氣、法庭、賓果、定存、寵物、共同記帳與專案
  • 推播通知 Token、內購診斷紀錄

刪除後，你的另一半會回到未配對狀態。資料會立即從資料庫移除；雲端供應商的備份會在 30 天內輪替刪除。

可能保留的資料

  • 訂閱付款紀錄由 Apple App Store／Google Play 依其規定保存，LoveBank 無法刪除。若要停止扣款，請在商店的「訂閱」頁面取消訂閱。

Delete your LoveBank account and data

App: LoveBank: Couples Love Bank (developer: LoveBank)

Option 1: Delete in the app (takes effect immediately)

  1. Open LoveBank and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and all data and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (no app needed)

Email zhichun0410@gmail.com from the address you registered with, using the subject "LoveBank account deletion request". After confirming you own the account, we will delete it within 7 business days and reply to let you know.

Data that is deleted

  • Account data: email, nickname, avatar and sign-in credentials
  • Everything in your shared vault: points, transactions, Love Menu, vouchers, red packets, time capsules, mood weather, Love Court, bingo, deposits, pets, shared expenses and projects
  • Push notification tokens and purchase diagnostic logs

Your partner will be unpaired. Data is removed from our database immediately; cloud provider backups rotate out within 30 days.

Data that may be kept

  • Subscription payment records are kept by the Apple App Store / Google Play under their own policies and cannot be deleted by LoveBank. To stop billing, cancel the subscription in the store's Subscriptions page.
