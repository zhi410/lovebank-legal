隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

LoveBank（以下稱「本服務」或「我們」）重視你的隱私。本政策說明當你使用 LoveBank 行動應用程式時，我們如何蒐集、使用、儲存與刪除你的資料。

1. 我們蒐集的資料

  • 帳號資料：Email、加密後的密碼、你設定的暱稱與頭像 Emoji。
  • 情侶金庫資料：配對邀請碼、紀念日、虛擬點數餘額、信用額度、自訂價目表、交易明細、審核紀錄與調額申請（含你填寫的理由文字）。
  • 共同記帳資料（VIP）：你與伴侶輸入的支出項目名稱、金額、幣別、記帳當時的匯率、自訂分類、付款者、分攤方式、日期、備註與結清紀錄。我們不會取得你的銀行帳戶、信用卡或任何真實支付資訊。
  • 見面計畫：你們設定的下次見面日期、見面夢想標題與見面基金點數。
  • 時區與語言偏好：App 會讀取裝置設定中的時區名稱（例如 Asia/Taipei，不是位置資訊）與你選擇的介面語言，用來向伴侶顯示你的當地時間，並在你的深夜時段將推播靜音送達、以你的語言發送通知。
  • 推播通知 Token：你允許通知後，Apple 會核發一組裝置推播 Token，我們用它向你的裝置傳送 App 通知。Token 只存在伺服器端，你的伴侶無法讀取；登出或刪除帳號時即移除。
  • 訂閱資料：透過 RevenueCat 取得的訂閱狀態與到期日。我們不會取得或儲存你的信用卡號等付款資訊，付款由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理。
  • 技術資料：為維持服務運作所必要的連線紀錄（例如 IP 位址、請求時間），由我們的雲端服務供應商處理。

我們不蒐集你的位置、通訊錄、相片，也不使用廣告追蹤或跨 App 追蹤。

2. 資料使用目的

  • 提供帳號登入、情侶配對、點數記錄與即時同步等核心功能。
  • 讓你與已配對的另一半看到共享金庫中的資料，並計算共同記帳的分攤與結清金額。
  • 確認 VIP 訂閱狀態並於情侶雙方間共享。
  • 傳送審核申請、紅包、額度與見面基金等推播通知；依收件人的時區於深夜靜音送達。
  • 回覆你的客服需求、維護服務安全與防止濫用。

3. 資料分享對象

你的情侶金庫資料只會分享給與你配對的另一半。我們不會出售你的個人資料。為提供服務，我們委託下列處理者：

  • Supabase（資料庫與身分驗證，託管於 Amazon Web Services 新加坡資料中心）
  • RevenueCat（訂閱狀態管理）
  • Apple / Google（App 內購付款處理）
  • Apple Push Notification service（推播通知傳送：裝置 Token 與通知內容）

共同記帳的即時匯率由 App 直接向公開匯率服務 open.er-api.com 查詢，查詢內容僅為幣別代碼（例如 TWD），不含任何個人資料或帳目內容。

除法律要求外，我們不會向其他第三方揭露你的資料。

4. 資料儲存與安全性

  • 雲端託管位置：資料儲存於 Supabase 雲端資料庫，底層為 Amazon Web Services（AWS）亞太（新加坡）區域 ap-southeast-1。託管供應商 Supabase 已通過 SOC 2 Type II 安全認證。更換手機時，登入原帳號即可從雲端還原資料。
  • 雙人資料隔離：資料庫啟用資料列層級安全（Row Level Security, RLS）。點數、信用額度、交易明細、共同記帳、盲盒與保單等資料，僅限同一共享金庫的你與伴侶讀取，其他使用者無法存取；所有點數異動皆由伺服器端驗證。
  • 加密：所有資料以 TLS 加密連線（HTTPS）傳輸；雲端靜態資料以 AES-256 加密儲存。
  • 不涉及真實金融資料：本服務不會索取、儲存或串接任何真實銀行帳號、信用卡號或密碼；VIP 訂閱付款由 Apple App Store / Google Play 處理。
  • 保存期間：我們在你的帳號存續期間保存資料；帳號刪除後，相關資料將立即自資料庫移除（供應商備份依週期於有限期間內輪替刪除）。

5. 刪除帳號與你的權利

  • 你可隨時在 App 內「我的 → 帳號管理 → 永久刪除帳號與所有關聯資料」自行刪除帳號。刪除後，你的帳號、個人資料以及你所屬共享金庫中的價目表與交易明細將永久刪除，另一半將回到未配對狀態。
  • 你可隨時「解除情侶綁定」，共享金庫資料將一併刪除。
  • 你可在 App 內修改暱稱等個人資料，或來信要求查詢、更正或刪除資料。
  • 刪除帳號不會自動取消 App Store / Google Play 的訂閱，請至商店的訂閱管理頁取消。

6. 兒童隱私

本服務不適用於未滿 13 歲之兒童（或你所在地區法律規定之更高年齡），我們不會故意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

7. 政策變更

若本政策有重大變更，我們會在 App 或本網頁公告。變更後繼續使用本服務，即表示你同意更新後的政策。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

LoveBank (“the Service”, “we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and delete your data when you use the LoveBank mobile app.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email, hashed password, your nickname and avatar emoji.
  • Couple vault data: invite code, anniversary, virtual point balances, credit limits, custom menu items, transaction history, approvals and limit-change requests (including any reason text you enter).
  • Shared expense data (VIP): expense titles, amounts, currencies, the exchange rate at the time of entry, custom categories, payer, split method, dates, notes and settlement records that you and your partner enter. We do not access your bank accounts, cards or any real payment information.
  • Meet-up plans: the next meet-up date, goal title and meet-up fund points you set.
  • Time zone & language: the app reads your device’s time zone name (e.g. Asia/Taipei — this is not location data) and your chosen app language, to show your partner your local time, deliver notifications silently during your late-night hours, and send notifications in your language.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, Apple issues a device push token that we use to deliver app notifications to your device. It is stored server-side only, is never visible to your partner, and is removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Subscription data: subscription status and expiry date obtained via RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your card number or payment details; payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • Technical data: connection logs necessary to operate the Service (e.g. IP address, request time), processed by our cloud providers.

We do not collect your location, contacts or photos, and we do not use advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. How We Use Data

  • To provide sign-in, couple pairing, point tracking and real-time sync.
  • To let you and your paired partner see your shared vault and calculate shared-expense splits and balances.
  • To verify VIP subscription status and share it between both partners.
  • To send push notifications for requests, red packets, limits and the meet-up fund, delivered silently during the recipient’s late-night hours.
  • To respond to support requests, keep the Service secure and prevent abuse.

3. Sharing

Your vault data is shared only with your paired partner. We never sell personal data. We use these processors to run the Service:

  • Supabase (database and authentication, hosted on Amazon Web Services in Singapore)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Apple / Google (in-app purchase processing)
  • Apple Push Notification service (notification delivery: device token and notification content)

Live exchange rates for shared expenses are fetched by the app directly from the public service open.er-api.com. Only a currency code (e.g. TWD) is sent — no personal data or expense details.

We do not disclose your data to other third parties unless required by law.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Where data is hosted: data is stored in a Supabase cloud database running on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the Asia Pacific (Singapore) region, ap-southeast-1. Our hosting provider, Supabase, is SOC 2 Type II certified. When you switch phones, sign in to restore your data from the cloud.
  • Couple-only isolation: the database enforces Row Level Security (RLS). Points, credit limits, transactions, shared expenses, gacha cards and insurance policies can only be read by you and your partner in the same shared vault — no other user can access them. Every balance change is validated on the server.
  • Encryption: all data is transmitted over TLS-encrypted connections (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest with AES-256.
  • No real financial data: the Service never asks for, stores or connects to real bank accounts, card numbers or passwords. VIP subscriptions are paid through the Apple App Store / Google Play.
  • Retention: we keep data while your account exists. When you delete your account, related data is removed from our database immediately (provider backups rotate out within a limited period).

5. Account Deletion & Your Rights

  • Delete your account anytime in the app: “Me → Account → Permanently delete account and all data”. This permanently deletes your account, profile, and the menu items and transactions of the shared vault you belong to; your partner returns to an unpaired state.
  • You can “Unpair” at any time, which deletes the shared vault.
  • You can edit your profile in the app, or email us to access, correct or delete your data.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel App Store / Google Play subscriptions. Please cancel in your store subscription settings.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or a higher age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

7. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app or on this page. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
